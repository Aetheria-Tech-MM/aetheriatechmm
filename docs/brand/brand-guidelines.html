Aetheria Tech

The Orbit A mark: an open ring for the company's reach, an A for Aetheria, and an orbiting dot for the product that carries it. This sheet covers the mark, colour, the product family, and the files to use.

The mark

One mark, four colourways

Use the full-colour mark wherever colour is available. Switch to mono for single-colour print, embossing, stickers, and engraving.

Strokes are outlined to filled paths in every exported file, so print vendors and design tools render it identically.

Lockups

Horizontal and stacked

Horizontal is the default (site header, documents, email signatures). Use stacked for square spaces such as slide title cards and stickers.

Minimum sizes

Below 16 px, use the favicon tile (onyx square), not the bare mark. In print, the lockup's minimum width is 25 mm.

Product family

Where the endorsement goes

  • Product website and docs footers
  • Transactional email footers
  • App "About" screens and store listing descriptions
  • Invoice and receipt PDF footers

Where it doesn't

  • App icons, favicons, and splash screens (product mark only)
  • Next to the product logo at equal size
  • Inside product UI chrome such as nav bars and toolbars
Usage

Do and don't

  • Keep clear space of one dot diameter around the mark
  • Use the dark-ground files on onyx or photography darker than 60%
  • Use mono when only one ink is available
  • Use the endorsement dot colour only for that product
  • Add glows, gradients, shadows, or outlines to the mark
  • Close the ring gap or move the dot out of it
  • Add a crossbar to the A or rotate the mark
  • Recolour the ring, or put amber ink on an amber ground
